Terms and Conditions

Last updated: August 25, 2026

1. Overview

JobPortal connects graduates seeking employment with employers looking to hire. By creating an account, you agree to the terms below.

2. Job Postings and Moderation

Every job posted by an employer is reviewed by our team before it appears publicly on the Listings page. Jobs are held in a "Pending Review" state until approved. We reserve the right to reject any posting that:

  • Requests upfront payment, fees, or "training materials" from applicants
  • Lists an unreasonable salary for the stated role or experience level
  • Has a title that does not match its description
  • Lacks basic company information

JobPortal does not guarantee the legitimacy of every employer or job listing. Our review process filters out obvious scams and low-effort postings but is not a substitute for your own due diligence.

3. Account Deletion

You may delete your account at any time from your profile settings. What happens to your data depends on your account type:

If you are a Graduate:

Your account and profile are removed. Applications you previously submitted are not deleted outright — they are anonymized. Your name, photo, CV, and cover note are removed from each application, but the record that an application was made (including its outcome, if an employer already responded) is kept. This preserves accurate hiring records for employers who reviewed your application. You will appear as "Deleted User" on any employer's Applicants page for jobs you previously applied to.

If you are an Employer:

Your account and all jobs you have posted are permanently deleted. If graduates had already applied to your jobs, those applications and jobs are removed from their dashboard, and their application statistics (total, pending, accepted, rejected) are recalculated automatically to reflect the change.

4. Account Suspension

We may suspend an account that violates these terms, including posting fraudulent jobs, requesting upfront payments from applicants, or abusing other users. A suspended account cannot log in or access the platform. You will be shown the reason for suspension if one was provided.

5. User Responsibilities

  • Provide accurate information when creating your account and profile
  • Never request or offer payment as a condition of applying to or offering a job
  • Do not upload files (CVs, logos) that are not what they claim to be
  • Report suspicious job postings or behavior to our team

6. Limitation of Liability

JobPortal is a platform connecting graduates and employers. We are not a party to any employment agreement made between users and are not liable for the conduct of any employer or graduate on the platform.

7. Changes to These Terms

We may update these terms from time to time. Continued use of JobPortal after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated terms.

8. Contact

Questions about these terms? Reach out via our Contact page.
