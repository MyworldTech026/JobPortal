Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 25, 2026

1. What This Policy Covers

This policy explains what information JobPortal collects, how it's used, who else can see it, and what choices you have. It applies to graduates, employers, and anyone browsing the site without an account.

2. Information We Collect

If you create a Graduate account:

  • Full name, email address, and phone number
  • Field of study
  • Profile photo (optional)
  • CV/resume file (PDF, DOC, or DOCX)
  • Cover notes you write when applying to a job

If you create an Employer account:

  • Company name, contact email, and website
  • Company logo (optional)
  • Industry and company description
  • The content of jobs you post

If you sign in with Google:

We receive your name, email address, and profile photo directly from Google. We never see or store your Google password.

Automatically collected:

Basic account activity, such as when your account was created and when you last logged in.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To show your job applications to employers you apply to, and applicant details to the employer for that specific job
  • To display job listings and company information publicly, once approved
  • To review new job postings for legitimacy before they go live
  • To notify you of relevant updates, like an application status change or a new job pending review (for admins)
  • To investigate reports of abuse, scams, or fraudulent listings

4. Who We Share Information With

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only as needed for the platform to function:

  • Firebase (Google) — handles account authentication and stores your profile, application, and job data.
  • Cloudinary — stores uploaded files: profile photos, company logos, and CVs.
  • Other users — an employer sees a graduate's application details (name, CV, cover note) only for jobs that graduate applied to. A graduate sees an employer's public company info and job listings.

5. How Long We Keep Your Data

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, what happens next depends on your account type — this is also covered in our Terms and Conditions:

  • Graduates: your profile is deleted. Applications you previously submitted are anonymized rather than deleted outright — your name, photo, CV, and cover note are removed from them, but the record that an application occurred is kept so employers retain accurate hiring history.
  • Employers: your profile and all jobs you posted are permanently deleted, along with associated applications.

6. Your Choices

  • You can update your profile information at any time from your account settings.
  • You can delete your account at any time — see Section 5 above for what that means for your data.
  • You can control what appears in your public profile (e.g. optional fields like a bio or company description).

7. Local Storage

JobPortal uses your browser's local storage to remember basic display preferences, such as which type of account you were last signed in as, so the site can load faster. This is not used for tracking or advertising and can be cleared at any time through your browser settings.

8. Security

Passwords are never stored or visible to us — authentication is handled entirely by Firebase. Access to your data within the platform is restricted based on your account role and ownership of that data.

9. Children's Privacy

JobPortal is intended for graduates and employers and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under the age required to hold a job in their jurisdiction.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of JobPortal after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Reach out via our Contact page.
